Famous Games

Minecraft is a 3D sandbox game created by Mojang Studios. You can create many types of buildings or challenge yourself and survive a day in survival mode.

Tetris is a 2D tile stacking game where you stack blocks into lines to score more points.

Grand Theft Auto V is a open world game where players could roleplay as criminals or polices. They could also choose to play the story mode to learn about the lore of the game.
